Arduino Mega → Ableton touch MIDI instrument v5

MIDI bridge with live controls, touch calibration and sensitive A0 diagnostics. Single HTML file. Use Chrome or Edge, not Safari.

1. Connect the Mac MIDI bus

Mac: Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio → double-click IAC Driver → turn on Device is online. Keep Ableton open.

Click Enable / check MIDI to request browser MIDI access.

2. Test Ableton, without the Arduino

Click Play 8-note test. Each note will be sent on MIDI channel 1 to the selected output, and you will see a count here. In Ableton, the MIDI Track In indicator should flash, even if no instrument is loaded.

Close the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor and any other serial bridge before connecting. The Mega often resets when the serial port opens. This bridge expects raw MIDI bytes sent with Serial.write(...), not text from Serial.print(...).

Upload Mega_Touch_Random_MIDI_v5.ino. The Arduino starts silent and automatically calibrates its resting voltage on startup. You can recalibrate at any time with your fingers off the contacts.

Controls are ready. Connect the Arduino to activate them.

Sensor check: The firmware field must say v5 verified, and Sensor reports received must increase about 10 times each second. If they do, look at RAW A0 while touching the contacts. If RAW A0 stays at zero, check the wiring and resistor, not Ableton. Click Test MIDI from Arduino to check the serial return path independently of the sensor. The bridge filters diagnostic messages so they never reach Ableton.

For two-pad sensing: connect one pad to Arduino 5V through 1 MΩ, connect the second pad to A0, and connect another 1 MΩ from A0 to GND. Touch both pads together. Merely touching an unconnected A0 input is not a reliable voltage sensor. Do not exceed 5V on A0.
